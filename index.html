Why choose Reliant?

HVOF process data is still captured by hand in many plants. Reliant replaces scattered logs with a single platform that records, analyzes and certifies each recuperation.

  • Centralized information for operators, supervisors and quality engineers.
  • End-to-end traceability of every component and work order.
  • Fault diagnosis based on real process data.
  • Verifiable quality evidence for Asset Owners.
  • Less dependence on manual records.

How it works

From sign-up to certified recuperation in four steps

  1. 1

    Create your organization

    Sign up as a Recuperation Supplier or an Asset Owner and invite your team with the right roles.

  2. 2

    Set up systems and recipes

    Register your HVOF systems, subsystems, clients, components and spray recipes with their parameter bands.

  3. 3

    Run and monitor sessions

    Open a recovery order, start a session and watch readings live. Deviations open fault cases automatically.

  4. 4

    Certify and share

    Analyze the session, generate the quality certificate and share the evidence with the Asset Owner.
